## 3. Automation & AI page

### Title
Automation & AI

### Intro
I use AI and automation to remove repetitive work, improve consistency and make complex processes easier to understand. The technology matters, but the workflow around it matters just as much.

### Section: How I use AI

AI supports several parts of my work:

- Research and information organization
- Product and system architecture
- Prototyping and interface exploration
- Software development and code review
- Documentation and technical communication
- Workflow automation and repetitive operations
- Testing, comparison and iteration

I do not treat generated output as finished work. I review, test and adapt it to the actual context of the project.

### Section: My working principles

**Human judgment stays in the loop.** AI can suggest, compare and accelerate. Responsibility for the result remains with the professional.

**Privacy is part of the design.** Sensitive data should not be sent to a model without a clear reason, adequate controls and the necessary authorization.

**Automation should be useful.** I automate a process when it reduces friction, improves reliability or gives people more time for work that requires judgment.

**Efficiency must be measurable.** In suitable workflows, I work toward at least a 30% reduction in repetitive effort. Project-specific results may be higher, but I do not present one result as a universal promise.

### Section: AI as a force multiplier

My advantage is not simply knowing how to use an AI tool. It is knowing where AI helps, where it creates risk and where experience is still essential. Years of design and development practice help me ask better questions, detect weak output and make decisions that fit the project.

## 6. Recommended project summaries

### DataWise
An AI-enabled, multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to unify data, automate processes and let users interact with systems through natural language. I worked across product definition, UX/UI, full-stack architecture, AI modules, governance controls and deployment. A solo delivery reached a working platform in approximately four months, compared with an estimated six to nine months for a conventional team approach. Results depend on project scope and workflow.

### SEMAD | Goiás Parks VR
An immersive 360° VR experience covering seven state parks in Goiás. The project combined field capture, spatial audio, 3D mascots, a local multi-headset application and synchronized sensory effects such as scent, wind, water and heat. The 30-day exhibition received more than 10,000 visitors.

### ABAL / COP30 — 3D Anamorphic Projection
An anamorphic projection that translated aluminum production processes into a visual narrative for a large-scale event environment. I created the 3D and post-production pipeline with Blender, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve.

### Glowtech USA
An integrated brand and digital experience for solar panels designed for golf carts. The work included brand identity, a 3D mascot, motion graphics, commercials, an SEO-focused website and an interactive mobile application with 3D visualization and energy simulation.

## Automação e IA

Uso IA e automação para reduzir tarefas repetitivas, melhorar a consistência e tornar processos complexos mais fáceis de entender. A tecnologia importa, mas o fluxo de trabalho criado ao redor dela importa tanto quanto.

### Como uso IA

- Pesquisa e organização de informações
- Arquitetura de produtos e sistemas
- Prototipação e exploração de interfaces
- Desenvolvimento e revisão de software
- Documentação e comunicação técnica
- Automação de fluxos e operações repetitivas
- Testes, comparação e iteração

Não considero um resultado gerado por IA como trabalho final. Eu reviso, testo e adapto o conteúdo ao contexto do projeto.

### Meus princípios de trabalho

**O julgamento humano permanece no processo.** A IA pode sugerir, comparar e acelerar. A responsabilidade pelo resultado continua sendo do profissional.

**Privacidade faz parte do projeto.** Dados sensíveis não devem ser enviados a um modelo sem motivo claro, controles adequados e autorização necessária.

**Automação precisa ser útil.** Automatizo quando isso reduz atrito, melhora a confiabilidade ou libera tempo para atividades que exigem julgamento.

**Eficiência precisa ser medida.** Em fluxos adequados, trabalho com uma meta de redução de pelo menos 30% no esforço repetitivo. Os resultados podem ser maiores em projetos específicos, mas não apresento um resultado isolado como promessa universal.
